Implementation details

  • One HTML file contains the layout, Canvas 2D artwork, styles, and animation code.
  • The sluice controls water flow and wheel speed; two linked gear stages drive the millstones at a 20:1 ratio.
  • Grain feed, pause, presets, stage focus, and the power-path overlay update the scene and live readouts.
  • Power and flour figures are illustrative estimates based on a 3.2 m head and 46% assumed efficiency.
