Prompt & implementation

Original prompt

create landscape in 3D interactive view, using blender 3D model hosted in standalone html: A forest with a lake in the center. In the middle of the lake, an abandoned house. Around the lake, a flower-filled French village. Paths connecting the important points.

How it was built

Blender scene
Procedural geometry created in Blender 3.6 with Python. A fixed random seed (17) makes the landscape reproducible. The scene includes a central lake, an overgrown island house, a plank bridge, French cottages, a chapel, a fountain square, flower gardens, lavender beds, woodland and connecting paths.
Geometry and materials
Boxes, cylinders, tapered cones, faceted foliage and path ribbons form the scene. Geometry is combined into 44 material batches to reduce browser draw calls. Roof tiles, shutters, flower boxes, climbing roses and boarded windows provide the architectural detail.
Model export
The editable scene is saved as lac-des-fleurs.blend and exported as landscape.glb, a binary glTF model with meshes and materials.
Standalone HTML
The GLB model is embedded as Base64. Three.js 0.169.0, its glTF loader, camera controls and interface code are bundled into the same HTML file with esbuild. The landscape opens from disk without a server, internet connection or external assets.
Rendering
Three.js renders the Blender model with WebGL, soft directional shadows, hemisphere lighting, atmospheric fog and ACES tone mapping. A physical water material uses animated shader normals to suggest gentle ripples.
Interaction
OrbitControls provides orbit, zoom and pan with mouse and touch input. Five camera viewpoints, clickable landmark labels, an automatic orbit and a guided tour help explore the scene. The panorama adapts to narrow screens.
Lighting and accessibility
Daylight, golden hour and blue hour change the sky, sunlight, ambient light and water color. Controls have accessible labels and keyboard focus. Camera moves and water animation respect reduced-motion preferences.
Source structure
model/create_landscape.py generates the Blender scene. viewer.js handles rendering and interaction. page.html defines the interface. build.mjs embeds the exported model and bundles everything into dist/index.html.
