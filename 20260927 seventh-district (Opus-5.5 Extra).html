第七区 下層BRIEF & BUILD NOTES · SEVENTH DISTRICT LOWER LEVEL · v1

The brief

“Seventh District: Lower Level” A massive elevated structure cuts across the submerged lower-level city. Shanties cling to and pile up on the pillars like parasites, while holographic koi carp float in the air. Up above, police hovercars sweep the streets with searchlights. What you can do in the browser ・Switch between 7 surveillance cameras to peek into the city ・Track residents / Fly a drone / Ride the maglev and take in the view ・Toggle the atmosphere (acid rain / smog / blackout / dawn) ・Overdensity levels 1–5, where pillar houses keep stacking up higher and higher ・Thermal imaging camera, full screen The buildings, the sign text, the residents, the cars, the rain, the lights—everything generated with code.

How the brief maps to the build

Brief itemWhere it lives
Elevated structure over a flooded cityAn 840 m deck, 8 m thick, 46 m above the water on 13 pillars. It carries 4 traffic lanes, sidewalks and the maglev guideway. The water is a mirror-rendered plane with rain rings.
Shanties on the pillarsRings of boxes stacked layer by layer around each pillar, with galleries every third layer. Pods hang from the deck girders, and towers climb the deck edge.
Holographic koi10 procedural koi (tapered body, forked tail, fins). The body wave, scanlines, rim glow and glitch slices all run in the shader. Each fish swims an elliptical loop.
Police hovercars + searchlights3 cars on figure-eight paths. Each carries a visible light cone and a real spotlight that lights walls, water and rain. A beam stops where it hits the deck.
7 surveillance camerasFixed positions with a slow PTZ sweep. Drag to take manual control; the sweep resumes after 6 seconds.
Track / drone / maglevTrack follows the nearest walking resident and shows a dossier. Drone launches from the current view. Maglev puts you in the Line 7 cab.
AtmospheresFour presets fade over about 2 s: fog, sky, rain, wind, window power, sign power, hologram stability and bloom.
Overdensity 1–5Pillar stacks reach 13 → 21 → 30 → 39 → 46 m. Edge towers rise up to 86 m, past the deck. The village gains storeys and the population grows from 102 to 190.
Thermal / full screenEvery material outputs a heat value when thermal is on. An ironbow palette maps it to colour. Full screen uses the browser API.

Implementation

Stack

  • One HTML file. Three.js r128 is loaded from cdnjs. All scene code is plain JavaScript.
  • No image, model or font files are used for the city. Every texture is painted onto a canvas at load.
  • Fonts: DotGothic16 for the kanji signs and HUD place names, Chakra Petch for Latin signs, Share Tech Mono for the telemetry.

Procedural generation

  • Texture atlas. Two 2048 px canvases hold 64 cells each. One is the wall colour and the other the window glow. Cells cover concrete, corrugated metal, 32 shanty walls, 12 tower facades, deck panels, roofs, planks and train windows. Windows get a random light colour, curtains, bars, silhouettes and AC units.
  • Sign atlas. 32 horizontal and 32 vertical signs: lightboxes, boarded neon and bare neon tubes. Text is set in canvas with glow passes, e.g. 居酒屋 七番, 義体クリニック, 質屋 PAWN, 酸性雨 注意.
  • Geometry. A small builder merges every box, cylinder and sign into a few big meshes, so the static city is one draw call. Each vertex carries its atlas cell, tint, a per-building seed and a heat value.
  • Seeded randomness. Each pillar has its own seed and the random calls don't depend on height. Raising overdensity therefore keeps the lower floors in place and adds floors on top.
  • Residents. Up to 200 instanced figures walk boardwalks, crosswalks, pillar galleries and the skyway sidewalks. Their legs and arms swing in the vertex shader. About 40 % carry LED umbrellas.

Rendering pipeline (per frame)

  • Reflection pass at half resolution, from a camera mirrored below the water line. The water samples it with wave and rain-ripple distortion, which gives the long neon streaks.
  • Main pass into a 4× multisampled half-float target, where supported, so neon can exceed white.
  • Bloom: threshold, then two widening Gaussian blurs at quarter resolution.
  • Final grade: chromatic fringe, tone curve, scanlines, grain, vignette, water drops on the camera lens in rain, and static bursts when you switch feeds. In thermal mode an ironbow palette replaces the colour grade.
  • Lighting is one shared shader library: hemisphere and ambient light, a low neon bounce field, four spotlights (three police, one maglev headlight) and height fog.
  • Rain is 14,000 line segments that wrap around the camera entirely on the GPU, so it costs no CPU per frame.
  • If frames get slow, the render scale drops automatically and climbs back when there is headroom.

Thermal model

Heads read hottest (1.0), then bodies, AC units and hovercar thrusters, lit windows and car engines. Walls, concrete and water are cold. The koi and umbrellas are projections and emit no heat, so they vanish in thermal.

Blackout logic

The grid drops to about 10 % of windows, now candle-warm and flickering, and 7 % of signs. Most street lamps go dark. The holo-koi stutter on backup power, and the searchlights get brighter.

Assumptions

  • The district is fictional, so there were no reference photos to match. Scale follows real infrastructure: the deck sits about 46 m up and the floors are about 3 m tall.
  • The HUD clock shows your local time with the year set to 2089.
  • Full screen depends on the viewer. Some embedded or phone views refuse it and show a notice.
