How it's made

  • Every frame is a pure function of time t: the same code runs in this page (real time, WebGL2 + Canvas 2D) and in a headless frame-exact renderer that wrote the MP4.
  • Pipeline: Canvas 2D vector layer + WebGL2 scene layer → float16 compose → 6-level dual-filter bloom → chromatic aberration, grade, vignette → temporal accumulation (4–16 sub-frames per frame at a 180° shutter = true motion blur and super-sampling) → film grain + dither.
  • Fourier: the outline of the glyph π is traced (Moore-neighbour), resampled by arc length and transformed with a DFT (221 coefficients); circles are sorted by amplitude, the 96 largest are drawn.
  • Surfaces: the mesh is only a (u,v) grid. A space curve K(u) (blend of a circle and two torus knots, analytic K′ and K″) gets an exact Frenet frame in the vertex shader; the tube is S(u,v) = K + ρ(cos v N + sin v B) and iso-lines are anti-aliased with fwidth. Reflections come from an analytic studio environment.
  • Lorenz: σ=10, ρ=28, β=8/3 integrated with RK4; a 42,000-point ghost of the attractor, a 360-particle ensemble and two hero orbits started 10−5 apart; the scene clock is calibrated so they split 62 % of the way in.
  • Mandelbrot: reference orbit in float64 on the CPU, per-pixel delta orbit in fp32 with Zhuoran rebasing, smooth iteration colouring.
  • Score: synthesised sample-by-sample in JavaScript at 144 BPM — melody = digits of π mapped onto an A-minor pentatonic scale, hi-hat rhythms from Euclidean (Bjorklund) patterns, chaos-section hats from the logistic map r=3.9, a Shepard tone under the infinite zoom, and the square-wave partials in the Fourier scene are the same harmonics you see being added.
  • Type: Inter Tight, JetBrains Mono and STIX Two Text (SIL OFL), embedded in this file.
