Science behind the art

A descent through scale

The sequence and scale labels are grounded in biology and physics. The graphics are interpretive. The last scene changes the kind of description; it does not claim that a quantum field can be photographed.

What you are seeing

  • Cell · ~2 μm: a representative rod-shaped E. coli. Its chromosome occupies a nucleoid without a surrounding nuclear membrane.
  • Nucleoid · hundreds of nm: the circular chromosome folds into loops and supercoils. It is not a neat isolated ring.
  • B-form DNA · ~2 nm wide: a right-handed double helix. Adjacent base pairs rise about 0.34 nm; about 10.5 pairs make one turn. A pairs with T, and G with C.
  • Atoms · ~0.1 nm: a simplified phosphate–sugar part of the backbone. The phosphorus, oxygen, carbon, and hydrogen atoms and bonds are schematic; the two unbridged phosphate oxygens are depicted equivalently because of resonance.
  • Quantum fields: a conceptual rendering of field amplitudes and localized excitations, not literal ripples that can be viewed under a microscope.
