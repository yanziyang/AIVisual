Original prompt

create a self contained aesthetically pleasing, breathtaking animation of a bacterial cell. it should start with cell and then zoom into it at multiple scales, going into DNA and then further into DNA as atoms and finally ending at quantum fields. use javascript for it. be detailed and scientifically accurate. it should evoke awe.

Rendered entirely in a single self-contained HTML file — one canvas, one script, no libraries, no assets, no network calls. Every structure drawn here is generated procedurally at runtime: the peptidoglycan lattice, the B-form helix, the adenine–thymine pair, the electron orbitals, the nucleons and the quantum fields.

Every atom in this cell is a knot of excitation in a quantum field.

You have travelled from 10−6 metres to 10−35 metres — from a living bacterium to the seething vacuum. Matter is not made of tiny billiard balls. It is made of fields, and the cell is what fields do when they are left alone in warm water for four billion years.
