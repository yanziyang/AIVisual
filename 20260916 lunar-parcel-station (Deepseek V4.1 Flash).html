Lunar Parcel Station

A whimsical animated SVG delivery cycle — looping and clickable.

Click any parcel while it is on the ground to make the rover prioritize it. The full cycle repeats automatically: capsule approach and thruster puffs, leg extension, a dusty touchdown, unfolding cargo doors, a conveyor unloading four differently behaving parcels, the rover waking and collecting each one over uneven terrain, an airlock scan before every pickup, and finally equipment retraction and liftoff — with satellites, beacons and drifting dust keeping the sky alive.

The prompt

Generate a richly animated SVG of a whimsical lunar parcel station, using midnight plum, pale gold, chalk white, and muted coral. A delivery capsule enters along a curved flight path, fires small corrective thrusters, extends its landing legs, and touches down in a spreading cloud of moon dust. Cargo doors unfold, a conveyor extends, and differently shaped packages roll out with distinct bouncing and sliding behavior. A small rover wakes up, swivels its antenna, drives over uneven terrain with articulated suspension, and collects the packages with a telescoping robotic arm. An airlock scans each package with a moving light before opening to receive it. Clicking a parcel makes the rover prioritize it. End with the capsule retracting its equipment and lifting off, while orbiting satellites, blinking beacons, and drifting dust keep the background active. Make the entire delivery cycle repeat smoothly.
