About this visualization

Prompt
Show me how a jet engine works in an inline 3D cutaway, hosted in standalone html.
Implementation details
Self-contained HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Procedural 3D engine surfaces are projected onto Canvas 2D. Drag or use the arrow keys to orbit; scroll or use +/− to zoom. Animated blades and cool/hot airflow show the five stages, while the throttle changes airflow speed, density, and exhaust glow. The status badge reports CPU drawing and checks WebGL GPU availability separately.
