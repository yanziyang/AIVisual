About this pond

The prompt

create interactive fish pond in standalone html. Use open source Clearwater engine: https://github.com/Aureliengmz/clearwater

Additional instruction: “Show the prompt and implementation details in the html web page.”

The supplied image guides the pond’s rock border, green water, gravel, grass, and koi.

How it is implemented

Water engine
Clearwater by Aurélien / Lumaris, used and adapted under the MIT license. Clearwater’s WebGL2 engine is embedded directly in this file.
Water motion
A 128 × 128 FFT wave spectrum drives the surface, adapted from Clearwater’s original 256 × 256 spectrum to reduce rendering cost. Touch disturbances use Clearwater’s separate 256 × 256 wave-equation ripple simulation over a seven-meter patch.
Light and depth
Clearwater calculates refraction, Fresnel reflection, absorption, and underwater light caustics. This version uses a 64 × 64 refracted ray grid and a 512 × 512 caustics texture, with the original bloom and tone-mapping pipeline. Lens diffraction glare is disabled for the overhead pond.
Pond scenery
A custom fragment shader renders 18 irregular boulders with rounded fracture planes, displaced surfaces, mineral variation, moss, and wet edges. Another 90 smaller stones are scattered in clusters between the boulders and around the gravel bank. Grass blades, contact shadows, and seven floating lily pads complete the shore. Clearwater’s embedded 1024 × 1024 pebble texture is reused.
Garden reflections
Tree branches and foliage reflect in the moving surface. A deeper, green water column, a shelving bottom, and dappled tree and shoreline shadows create areas of changing visibility beneath the water.
Koi rendering
The koi have tapered, curved bodies, forked swimming tails, translucent pectoral and pelvic fins, scale rows, eyes, and several color varieties. Their bodies are intersected and shaded inside the refracted underwater ray, so the water distorts and tints the fish.
Koi behavior
JavaScript updates each koi’s position and heading. They wander, avoid nearby fish, turn away from the shore, and steer toward food. Feeding adds up to eight pellets per tap; fish eat nearby pellets, and uneaten food clears after 24 simulated seconds.
Controls
Ripple and feeding modes; drag to orbit; wheel or pinch to zoom; pause and reset view; breeze, morning / afternoon / golden-hour light, and three to ten koi.
Light presets
Click the current light at the top right to choose Morning, Afternoon, or Golden hour. The selected option is highlighted in both the picker and Settings. Morning and afternoon use different sun directions, with brighter afternoon light and warm, low golden-hour light. Changing the light also refreshes the underwater caustics while paused, without advancing the water or fish simulation.
Performance
Animation is capped at 30 frames per second with adaptive rendering resolution. Paused scenes redraw when the view or scene settings change. Simulation updates suspend in background tabs and while this About panel is open.
Standalone file
All JavaScript, shaders, styling, textures, this reference image, and the license are embedded. There are no libraries, network requests, or external asset dependencies. Open the HTML directly from disk.
Requirements
A browser with WebGL2 and floating-point render targets: EXT_color_buffer_float or EXT_color_buffer_half_float. Unsupported graphics show an error message.

Keyboard and accessibility

Tab to reach controls. With the pond focused: Enter ripples or feeds at the center, arrow keys orbit, + / − zoom, Space pauses or resumes, R resets the view, and F selects feeding. Escape closes a panel. The pond starts paused when your system prefers reduced motion.

Source structure

The delivered fish-pond.html is self-contained. The editable project uses pond-shell.html for the interface, pond-scene.glsl for the scenery and fish shader, and pond-behavior.js for behavior and controls. build_pond.py combines them with the unmodified upstream source and license saved in clearwater-source/.

Upstream source: Clearwater index.html. The embedded source was downloaded on 27 September 2026.
