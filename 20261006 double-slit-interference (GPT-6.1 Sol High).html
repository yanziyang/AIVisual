The double-slit experiment

When waves meet

Light emerging from each slit spreads out. Where the two waves arrive in step, they make a bright stripe; where they arrive out of step, they make a dark one.

About this visualization

Prompt
Visualize how light passing through two slits creates bright and dark stripes, host the animation in a standalone HTML.
Implementation details
  • Canvas draws incoming light, wavefronts spreading from both slits, and the brightness pattern on the screen.
  • Screen brightness follows the waves’ path difference using an interference term, cos²(πΔr/λ), with a broad single-slit envelope.
  • Wavelength and slit separation are adjustable. Select a point on the screen to see whether the waves reinforce or cancel. All code and styling are contained in this HTML file.
