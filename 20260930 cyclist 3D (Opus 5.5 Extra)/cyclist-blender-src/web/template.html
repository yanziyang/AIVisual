About this build

The rider and the bike are real Blender data: every mesh, the skeleton, the IK pedalling rig and the three animation loops were produced by Python scripts running Blender headless. The page loads that glTF and does the rest in three.js.

Modelled in Blender 3.6.23 LTS (bpy, headless)

  • Frame and fork: metaball tube chains. The element radius is solved from the metaball field integral, so tubes come out at their exact diameter and the joints blend like carbon lay-up. Then converted, smoothed and decimated, with paint seams cut along planes.
  • Geometry: 56 cm endurance road frame (73.5° seat, 73° head angle, 986 mm wheelbase), 700×26c tan-wall tyres on 45 mm carbon rims, bladed spokes (20 radial / 24 two-cross), disc rotors.
  • Drivetrain: 52/36 rings and 11–28 cassette as toothed solids. The chain is solved as a belt: tangent runs and wrap arcs around ring, cog and both jockey wheels give 108 links.
  • Cockpit: swept drop bar with a helical bar-tape ridge, hoods, lever blades, stem, spacers, head unit.
  • Rider: one metaball body of 636 bone-tagged elements, modelled in the riding pose. Skin weights come from each element's share of the field (bone-tagged), then get Laplacian smoothing. Kit seams are cut with planes (cuffs, hems, collar) and with iso-lines of the weight field (waist, hairline). Helmet shell with vents and solidify, wrap-around glasses, cycling shoes with cleats.
  • Rig: 24 deform bones and IK controls. Crank and pedal ankling are keyed. Foot IK has knee poles, hands are IK-pinned to the hoods, and pole angles are solved numerically. Three loops are baked with visual keying: Seated, Standing (bike rocks ±6.5°) and Coast.

Exported and played in three.js

  • glTF 2.0 / GLB. gltf-transform runs dedup, resample and meshopt compression (4.9 MB down to 1.36 MB), and the result is embedded in this file as base64.
  • The AnimationMixer runs phase-locked blends. Seated and standing share one crank phase, and coasting waits until the cranks are level, so the feet never slip on the pedals.
  • Chain links are instanced along the path exported from Blender and driven by the crank. The cassette and jockey wheels turn with the chain (the cassette freewheels when you coast). Wheels roll with road speed, with spoke-blur discs.
  • Gears shift automatically to hold your target cadence. Speed follows the gradient, the bike leans into bends (tan θ = v²κ/g), and on Auto the rider stands on climbs and coasts on descents.
  • Procedural valley road streamed in 64 m chunks, cloud sky shader, PMREM sky reflections, soft sun shadows, ACES tone mapping.
